Apple Harvester Drive System

Documenting the development of a robust drive system for an automated apple harvester.

Project Overview

This project involves the design and implementation of the drive system software for an automated apple harvester. The system focuses on achieving precise control and stability while navigating rough terrains.

Background

In 2023 Advanced Farm developed a robotic apple harvester. This harvester re-used a lot of our technical stack from strawberry harvest and allowed us to pick over 80,000 apples! I manage the software testing team at Advanced Farm but for this project I was responsible for all drive system related software.

Problem Statement

The need for this project came from a quick relization that we needed to stay centered in an apple trellis in order to pick effectively and reduce downtime. Being unable to stay centered introduces the following issues:

  • The work volume of our robots became limited or out of range.
  • Robots are more likely to snag when driving towards one side of the trellis.
  • The harvester is unable to correct it's steering angle and you risk damaging the orchard.

Technical Details

Before I jump into my approaches and design let me give you some insight into the system I had to become familiar with.

  • Control Systems: PID Control
  • Steering: Electric Differential, Ackermann Steering
  • Motors: I&W EMAC Motors
  • Controllers: Sevcon Gen4 Compact AC Controllers
  • Communication Protocols: CAN, CANOpen
  • Sensors: Encoders
  • Programming Languages: Python

Process

  • Phase 1: Commissioning the Sevcon Gen4 Compact AC Controllers
  • Phase 2: Designing and prototyping
  • Phase 3: Implementation and testing

Results

After implementing the drive system software, the apple harvester was able to navigate through apple trellises with precision and stability. The system was able to maintain a centered position within the trellis, reducing the risk of snagging and damage to the orchard.
